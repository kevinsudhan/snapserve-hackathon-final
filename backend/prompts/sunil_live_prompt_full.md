Which crops
[S17] (PMFBY) The scheme covers food crops such as cereals, millets and pulses, oilseeds, and annual commercial or horticultural crops.

What is covered
[S18] (PMFBY) The basic cover protects the standing crop from sowing until harvesting.
[S19] (PMFBY) The basic cover applies to yield loss from risks a farmer cannot prevent, such as drought, dry spells, flood and inundation.
[S20] (PMFBY) The basic cover also lists widespread pest and disease attack, landslide, natural fire from lightning, storm, hailstorm and cyclone.
[S21] (PMFBY) If the state has notified it, there is an add-on cover for when sowing or planting could not happen at all because of not enough rain or bad weather.
[S22] (PMFBY) There is a mid-season cover for bad seasons like floods, long dry spells and severe drought, where the harvest is expected to fall below half of normal.
[S23] (PMFBY) Post-harvest cover is available only for up to two weeks from harvesting, for crops left in the field to dry.
[S24] (PMFBY) The post-harvest cover is for damage by hailstorm, cyclone, cyclonic rain and unseasonal rain to a cut crop lying in the field.
[S25] (PMFBY) For post-harvest cover the crop must have been drying in the field in cut and spread or small bundled condition, up to fourteen days from harvest.
[S26] (PMFBY) Localized calamity cover is for hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloud burst and natural fire from lightning that hit single farms rather than the whole area.
[S27] (PMFBY) Inundation means standing water from natural rain or flood water that stays a long time and visibly damages the crop.
[S28] (PMFBY) The add-on covers only exist in a district if the state government has notified them for that season.
[S29] (PMFBY) Some states also add a cover for crop damage caused by wild animals, where they have chosen to offer it.
[S30] (PMFBY) The state government fixes the normal harvesting dates for each crop, and the post-harvest cover cannot be used for an event outside that period.

[S31] (PMFBY) Losses from war, nuclear risks, deliberate damage, and other things that could have been prevented are not covered.
